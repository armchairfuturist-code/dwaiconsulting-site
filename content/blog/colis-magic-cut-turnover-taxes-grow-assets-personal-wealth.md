When it comes to planning, small business owners often focus on cash flow, taxes, and employee benefits. But one often-overlooked strategy that impacts them all is Company-Owned Life Insurance (COLI).

#### What is COLI?

Company-Owned Life Insurance (COLI) is a policy purchased by a business on the lives of key employees, including owners. The company pays the premiums and is the beneficiary of the policy, meaning it receives the death benefit when the insured individual passes away. These policies can also accumulate cash value over time, providing financial flexibility.

#### Key Benefits of COLI for Small Business Owners

\1. Business Continuity & Succession Planning

If a key employee or owner passes away unexpectedly, a COLI policy provides a death benefit that can help the business stay afloat. It ensures that surviving partners or family members have the funds to cover expenses, repay debts, or buy out shares from an estate.

\2. Tax Advantages

Although the premiums cannot be deducted because the company is the beneficiary, the cash value growth of a COLI policy is tax-deferred. Additionally, the death benefit is generally tax-free to the business, providing a significant financial cushion.

\3. Lowering Employee Turnover

Many COLI policies can be structured to fund executive compensation programs, such as deferred compensation or retirement benefits. This can be a great way to retain top talent by offering long-term financial incentives.

\4. Becoming Your Own Bank

Over time, the COLI policy builds up cash value that the business can record as an asset. The company can borrow against or use this asset for future investments, expansions, or even emergency funding. You can sidestep traditional banking and access money without an approval process and usually at lower interest rates.

\5. Funding Buy-Sell Agreements

For businesses with multiple owners, COLI can be used to fund buy-sell agreements, ensuring that the remaining owners can purchase the deceased owner’s shares without depleting company reserves.

#### Is COLI Right for Your Business?

If you’re a small business owner looking to strengthen your company’s financial foundation while protecting key stakeholders, COLI might be a smart addition to your business strategy.
