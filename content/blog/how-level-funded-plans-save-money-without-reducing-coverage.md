Tired of paying more for health insurance every year without seeing better benefits?
If you're a small business owner, there's a smarter way to offer great coverage and take back control of your costs — it’s called level funding.

### What Is a Level-Funded Health Plan?

A level-funded plan gives you the predictability of fixed monthly payments with the potential to save money — or even get a refund at the end of the year.

Your monthly payment covers:

- Claims funding (for your employees’ actual care)
- Stop-loss insurance (to protect you from large claims)
- Admin fees (to handle billing, compliance, and customer service)

If your team uses fewer healthcare services than expected, you keep the savings.

### Why Small Businesses Are Making the Switch

Most small group plans under the ACA are priced based on age, location, and community averages — not your actual team.

That means if your workforce is:

- Mostly younger or healthy
- Male-dominant (statistically lower claims)
- Under 50 employees

...you're probably overpaying.

Level funding lets you break free from one-size-fits-all pricing and pay based on your own team's risk.

### What You Don’t Have to Give Up

Level funding doesn't mean cutting corners. You can still offer:

- Nationwide PPO networks
- Mental health and telehealth services
- Preventive care at no cost
- Plan design flexibility to match your company’s needs

And unlike traditional insurance, you get transparency into how your dollars are actually spent.

### Is It Right for You?

Level funding isn’t for everyone — but if your business qualifies, the savings can be significant.

You’ll know upfront if you qualify (based on your employee census), and there’s no pressure to switch unless it makes sense for you.
