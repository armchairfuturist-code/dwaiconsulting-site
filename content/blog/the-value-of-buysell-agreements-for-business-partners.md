Your business is your most valuable asset, but what happens if an owner unexpectedly leaves, becomes incapacitated, or passes away? Without a plan in place, business continuity can be at risk, leading to financial strain, disputes, or even the dissolution of the company. This is where a buy/sell agreement proves its worth.

#### What Is a Buy/Sell Agreement?

A buy/sell agreement is a legally binding contract that outlines what happens to a business owner's share of the company in case of a triggering event, such as death, disability, retirement, or voluntary departure. By establishing clear terms, it ensures a smooth transition of ownership and minimizes disruptions to operations.

#### The Benefits of a Buy/Sell Agreement

\1. Ensures Business Continuity

Without a structured plan, the sudden departure of an owner can create instability. A buy/sell agreement guarantees an orderly transition, keeping the business operational.

\2. Prevents Ownership Disputes

Without predefined terms, disagreements can arise among remaining owners or heirs. A buy/sell agreement eliminates uncertainty by clearly outlining ownership transition rules.

\3. Protects Financial Interests

A buy/sell agreement provides a method for valuing ownership stakes, ensuring a fair market price for both selling and remaining owners.

\4. Provides Liquidity for Heirs or Departing Owners

If an owner passes away or exits the business, their family may need financial support. A buy/sell agreement—often funded by life or disability insurance—ensures they receive fair compensation while the business remains stable.

\5. Restricts Ownership Transfers to Outside Parties

A buy/sell agreement ensures that shares stay within the business by granting existing owners the first right to purchase a departing partner’s share, preventing unwanted third-party involvement.

#### Common Buy/Sell Agreement Structures

- Cross-Purchase Agreement: Allows remaining owners to buy the departing owner’s share.
- Entity Purchase Agreement: The business itself repurchases the departing owner's shares.
- Hybrid Agreement: Combines aspects of both cross-purchase and entity purchase agreements for added flexibility.

#### How to Set Up a Buy/Sell Agreement

1. Consult a Business Attorney – Work with a professional to draft an agreement tailored to your business needs.

2. Establish a Valuation Method – Define how ownership stakes will be valued.

3. Secure Funding Mechanisms – Life insurance or other financial strategies can ensure funds are available for buyouts.

4. Define Triggering Events – Clearly outline the circumstances under which the agreement takes effect.

5. Review and Update Periodically – Adjust the agreement as the business evolves.

#### Final Thoughts

A buy/sell agreement is an essential tool for business owners seeking stability, financial security, and a smooth transition plan. By putting one in place, entrepreneurs can ensure their business and legacy remain protected.
