As a business owner, accessing capital can be a constant challenge. Traditional bank loans come with interest payments, strict lending requirements, and loss of financial control. But what if you could be your own bank? With Company-Owned Life Insurance (COLI), you can create a tax-advantaged funding source that gives you flexibility, liquidity, and financial security. Here’s how it works and why it might be a smart strategy for your business.

### What is COLI?

Company-Owned Life Insurance (COLI) is a permanent life insurance policy that a business purchases on key employees or owners. The company owns the policy, pays the premiums, and is the beneficiary. The policy accumulates cash value over time, which can be accessed tax-efficiently - often as early as year 2.

### How Can COLI Be Used Like a Bank?

1. Cash Value Accumulation: COLI policies build cash value that grows tax-deferred. This becomes a financial asset your business can leverage.

2. Tax-Free Loans: You can borrow against the cash value of the policy at favorable interest rates. Unlike traditional loans, these policy loans do not require credit approval and do not show up on your personal credit report.

3. No Loan Repayment Pressure: Unlike bank loans, policy loans do not have fixed repayment schedules. You can repay them on your terms or allow the loan balance to be deducted from the death benefit.

4. Tax-Efficient Withdrawals: In certain cases, cash value can be withdrawn tax-free up to the amount of premiums paid, providing liquidity without tax consequences.

### Practical Uses of COLI for Business Owners

- Financing Business Growth: Use policy loans to fund expansion, acquire new equipment, or invest in marketing without dealing with traditional lenders.
- Emergency Fund: The cash value serves as a reserve for unexpected expenses or downturns.
- Executive Compensation: COLI can be used to provide benefits to key employees, offering bonuses or deferred compensation.
- Exit Planning & Buy-Sell Agreements: The policy’s death benefit ensures liquidity to fund buyouts of partners or protect against financial instability in the event of an owner’s passing.

### Why COLI is a Better Alternative to Traditional Banking

- Control: You control the borrowing process, repayment terms, and access to funds.
- Tax Advantages: Tax-free growth, tax-free loans, and a tax-free death benefit provide significant advantages over conventional loans.
- Asset Protection: In many states, the cash value of life insurance enjoys creditor protection, shielding business assets.
- Guaranteed Liquidity: Unlike other business assets, COLI provides predictable and accessible cash when needed.

### Comparing COLI Loans to Bank Loans

When deciding whether to borrow from a COLI policy or a bank, consider the differences:

Which One is Better?

- COLI loans often have lower interest rates than bank loans, especially if the policy is structured properly.
- Unlike banks, COLI does not require credit approval, making access to capital easier.
- Policy loans allow cash value to continue growing, which can offset borrowing costs.
- Bank loans may have lower rates for businesses with excellent credit, but they require strict repayment schedules and collateral.

### Is COLI Right for You?

COLI is best suited for businesses with steady revenue that can afford to fund the policy over time. It requires careful planning, and working with an insurance professional is key to structuring the policy for maximum benefit.

### Final Thoughts

Being your own bank through COLI can transform the way you finance your business. With tax advantages, liquidity, and financial security, it provides a strategic alternative to traditional lending.
