A buy-sell agreement is designed to help protect business continuity when an owner dies, becomes disabled, retires, or unexpectedly exits the business. Without a clear funding strategy in place, surviving owners and family members can face significant financial and operational uncertainty during an already difficult situation.

Life insurance is commonly used to fund buy-sell agreements because it can provide immediate liquidity when it is needed most. Depending on the structure of the business and ownership goals, agreements may use cross-purchase or entity-purchase arrangements, each with different legal, tax, and operational considerations.

For closely held businesses, particularly partnerships and multi-owner companies, buy-sell planning can help preserve ownership stability, reduce conflict, and create a clearer path for succession and long-term business continuity.
